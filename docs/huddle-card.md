# The 7:00 huddle card

Ten minutes, standing at the board, crew leads present, Jack runs it (Jamie when Jack is out). The agenda is the default screen, top to bottom. Nobody sits, nobody types, nobody opens a laptop. Decisions 37 and 38.

| Clock | Zone | Say | Not this |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Answer line | Read it as written: "Five to act on now, four more today." If it names a Problem, the responder says who is calling and taps ACK. If line 2 says Stale, say so and run the huddle on those numbers. | Debugging the service at the huddle. |
| 0:15 | Needs you rows | Top to bottom. Each owner says a time out loud for their row: "Bauer, 21 days, first review: Henry by 4:30." A CREW row: the lead whose visit it was says when (a FIX is due end of the next business day). A HOLD row: Jack decides now or says when. Then read the names line. | Explaining why it is late. |
| 3:00 | Requires invoicing | The hero number, then each line: "Twelve jobs. Nothing done today waiting. Four first passes, Henry by 4:30. One second pass, Jack by 5." | Reading every job; Cannot invoice has them. |
| 3:30 | Crews | Each lead, one sentence: the NOW job and the next, plus anything the row cannot show (materials on the truck, access confirmed). | Crew leads reading Jobber off their phone. |
| 6:00 | Focus and win numbers | Say the focus line once. Read `READY 1st pass` and yesterday's plan kept, unanswered included. One spoken recognition for one person or crew. | Rankings, streaks, bonuses (decision 45). |
| 7:00 | Monday only | One-minute forecast from each crew lead: permits, materials, inspections this week (decision 39). | — |
| 10:00 | Stop | "That's the board." | Running over. |

## Rules of the huddle

- The board is read, not edited, during the huddle. Owner changes on rail lines are typed by Jamie after.
- A number nobody looked at for 30 days comes off the board (Jack's calendar, decision 38).
- Red means act today and always comes with a word. Amber means watch. Grey means someone else's clock.
- No incidents, no names on quality numbers, no ladder (decision 16). Those conversations are private and happen after.
- Monday only: the 7:00 huddle ends with a one-minute forecast from each crew lead (what this week needs: permits, materials, inspections). The office scorecard follows at 7:15 without the crews (decision 39).

## Before the first huddle on the wall

Run this on the 55-inch panel itself (or a 55-inch TV driven by the laptop with `?wall=1`); a laptop screen cannot stand in for it. Tape marks at 12 ft and 15 ft. Someone who did not build the board reads out, from each mark: the answer line, every ledger name with its age and owner, one status and why, the hero number, each crew's NOW job and status. Include both JA and JT rows. If a 56 px name fails at 15 ft, names drop to 42 (decision 36 as amended); fix brightness or type size, never the content. Repeat at 15:00 for glare.
