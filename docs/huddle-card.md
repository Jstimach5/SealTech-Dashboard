# The 7:00 huddle card

Ten minutes, standing at the board, crew leads present, Jack runs it (Jamie when Jack is out). The agenda is the default screen, top to bottom. Nobody sits, nobody types, nobody opens a laptop. Decisions 37 and 38.

| Clock | Zone | Say | Not this |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Footer dots | "Dots are green." If Jobber is amber or red, say so and run the huddle on yesterday's numbers; the banner already says when the last load was. | Debugging the service at the huddle. |
| 0:05 | NEEDS ATTENTION | Read each line oldest first: trigger word, name, days, initials. The initials own it by the end of the day. If it is Requires invoicing, say nothing more here; the rail covers it. | Explaining why it is late. |

| 8:00 | Counts strip | Say UNANSWERED. If it is above 0, the lead whose visit it was answers it in the Jobber app before the trucks roll. Say the focus line once. | Reading all eight numbers. |
| 9:00 | Recognition | One sentence, one person or crew, for something on the board (a week of READY first pass, a kept plan). Spoken, not written. | Rankings, streaks, bonuses (decision 45). |
| 10:00 | Stop | "That's the board." | Running over. |

## Rules of the huddle

- The board is read, not edited, during the huddle. Owner changes on rail lines are typed by Jamie after.
- A number nobody looked at for 30 days comes off the board (Jack's calendar, decision 38).
- Red means act today. Amber means watch. Grey means someone else's clock.
- No incidents, no names on quality numbers, no ladder (decision 16). Those conversations are private and happen after.
- Monday only: the 7:00 huddle ends with a one-minute forecast from each crew lead (what this week needs: permits, materials, inspections). The office scorecard follows at 7:15 without the crews (decision 39).

## Before the first huddle on the wall

Tape a mark at 12 ft. Someone who did not build the board reads out: every lane's NOW name and chip, the hero number, one rail row, one strip line. If any of those fail, fix type size or brightness, never the content (decision 36). Repeat at 15:00 for glare.
